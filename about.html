About UdyogSetu

Bridging industries. Connecting opportunities.

We’re building a connection platform for people looking to buy, sell, grow or invest in industrial businesses and assets — beginning in Madhya Pradesh.

Who we are

Industrial transactions are about more than finding a listing.

Behind every industrial opportunity is a business owner, a buyer, an investor or a team making an important decision. Finding the right counterparty can be difficult — especially when information is fragmented and conversations need to remain confidential.

UdyogSetu aims to make that first connection more structured. We listen to both sides, understand the broad requirement and help facilitate relevant conversations where there is a potential fit.

Our role is to create a thoughtful starting point for industrial business connections. Any transaction remains subject to independent verification, due diligence, definitive agreements and the parties’ own professional advice.

Our approach

Built around trust, clarity and intent.

We want every enquiry to begin with a clear understanding of what the person is trying to achieve.

Listen to both sides

We welcome enquiries from sellers as well as buyers and investors, and seek to understand each requirement on its own merits.

Respect confidentiality

Business-sensitive details should be shared carefully, with appropriate consent and access controls.

Qualify before connecting

We aim to understand the broad intent and suitability of an enquiry before facilitating an introduction.

Be transparent

We do not represent an opportunity as verified unless the relevant checks have actually been completed.

Where we're starting

From Madhya Pradesh, with a wider horizon.

Our initial focus is on industrial centres such as Indore, Pithampur, Dewas, Bhopal and Mandideep. As the network develops, we aim to build connections across more locations and sectors in India.
